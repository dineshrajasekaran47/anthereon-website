Team Context

Maintain technique standards across the entire team.

In team settings, athletes train in the same sport and drills. Anthereon makes every athlete visible during training, allowing coaches to maintain consistency, spot breakdowns early, and focus attention where it’s needed most.

  • Monitor all players performing the same drills
  • Compare movement quality across the team
  • Identify players compensating or fatiguing early
  • Make faster, more targeted coaching corrections
