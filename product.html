Anthereon Performance Gear

The Anthereon System

Anthereon brings artificial intelligence to human motion through connected wearables, adaptive intelligence, and a unified app.

Hardware

Lightweight wearables designed to move naturally with you.

Designed to move naturally with you while capturing how your body moves in real time.

Anthereon’s wearable units integrate seamlessly into training, following your movement without restricting motion or changing how you train.

  • Worn directly on the body
  • Comfortable during extended sessions
  • No cameras or external setup
  • Built for gym, field, court, and home
Bio-Intelligence

Movement intelligence built around the human body.

At the core of Anthereon is a bio-intelligence layer designed to understand how people move, not just how motion is recorded. It interprets control, coordination, stability, and timing to form a living model of movement that adapts to the individual.

Rather than enforcing rigid patterns, this intelligence learns how each body moves and responds, enabling guidance that feels precise, personal, and natural.

What it enables
  • Real-time motion correction as you move
  • Feedback adapted to individual mechanics and proportions
  • Consistent understanding of movement across training, sport, and recovery
Software

One app that works quietly in the background.

Select your workout or sport, go hands-free, and train as usual. Anthereon runs continuously, guiding and tracking movement without requiring constant interaction.

The app turns complex movement into simple understanding, before, during, and after training.

  • Select any workout or sport before you begin
  • Train hands-free without looking at a screen
  • Receive real-time haptic feedback for motion correction
  • Track technique, consistency, and performance over time

Hardware captures movement. Bio-intelligence interprets it. The app delivers guidance.
